01Who we are

Imaji is a photo editing app published by Kanda Apps ("we", "us").

Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL — required before submission]

02The short version

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Your photos stay on your device unless you tap one of the three AI tools.
  • When you do, that single photo is sent for processing and we do not keep it.
  • We do not perform facial recognition or build face templates.
  • You can use the editor without an account.

03What we collect

Photos you choose to edit. Imported photos are stored on your device. We

never scan or upload your photo library. Only the specific image you select is

ever touched, and it only leaves the device if you use an AI tool (§4).

Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address

and a user id. You can use the editor and the AI tools anonymously; an account

exists so your work isn't tied to one device.

Subscription status. If you subscribe, our payment provider tells us whether

your subscription is active. We never see your card details — payment is

handled entirely by Apple or Google.

AI usage records. For each AI operation we store the operation type

(background removal, object removal, upscale), a timestamp, and your user id, so

we can enforce monthly usage limits. These records do not contain your photo.

Diagnostics. If enabled in the build, we collect crash reports and error

diagnostics to fix bugs. These do not contain your photos.

We do not collect: contacts, location, health data, browsing history, device

advertising identifiers, or your photo library as a whole.

04AI features and third-party processing

This is the part most people want to understand, so we're being specific.

Three features send an image off your device for processing:

FeatureWhat it does
Remove BackgroundSeparates the subject from the background
Magic EraserRemoves an object you brush over and reconstructs what was behind it
UpscaleIncreases resolution

1. Nothing is sent until you tap one of those tools. Ordinary editing —

cropping, filters, exposure, colour — happens entirely on your device.

2. Before your first AI operation we ask for your explicit consent and explain

what will be sent and where.

3. The image is sent over an encrypted connection to our processing service,

which forwards it to our AI provider, fal.ai (fal.ai Inc.), who run the

models. Their privacy policy: https://fal.ai/privacy

4. The result comes back and is saved on your device.

  • We do not store your photos on our servers. The image is passed through

for processing, not retained by us.

  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not share your photos with anyone other than the processing provider

described above, and only for the operation you requested.

What we cannot control: fal.ai processes the image on their infrastructure

and applies their own retention practices. We chose a provider whose terms

support this use, but their handling is governed by their policy, not ours.

Before you upload: be thoughtful about images containing other people,

children, documents, or anything sensitive — the same care you would take with

any online service.

05Faces and biometric data

Imaji's AI tools perform image segmentation and enhancement. They separate

foreground from background, fill in removed regions, and add resolution.

We do not:

  • perform facial recognition or facial identification,
  • create, store or compare face templates, faceprints, or biometric identifiers,
  • attempt to determine who is in a photo,
  • match faces across photos or against any database.

A photo of a person is processed as an image, not as a biometric identifier. We

do not collect biometric information as defined by the Illinois Biometric

Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas CUBI Act, or comparable laws.

06AI-generated and AI-edited content

Results produced by the AI tools are labelled in the app. You can report a

result you believe is inappropriate from the screen where it appears.

Imaji does not generate images of people who don't exist, perform face swapping,

alter clothing, or produce imagery intended to deceive — and we do not intend to

add such features.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK/EU GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA in

California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your

data, and to object to certain processing.

To exercise any of these, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will respond within

the period required by law.

**We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context

behavioural advertising.**

Deleting your account removes your account record, subscription mirror and

usage records. Photos held on your device are removed when you delete the app.

09Children

Imaji is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect

personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with

personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. AI provider credentials are held only on

our server and never in the app. Database access is protected by row-level

security so one account cannot read another's records. No system is perfectly

secure, but we keep the amount of data we hold deliberately small.

11Changes

If we change this policy materially — particularly how AI processing works or

who processes your data — we will update this page and, where the change is

significant, tell you in the app.
