04AI features and what they do

AI Styles and AI Video are generative. They produce new imagery based on your photo. You should understand what that means before you rely on a result:

  • A result can change nearly everything except the subject's face — clothing, pose, background, lighting, era, and the medium the image appears to be made from. Costume and transformation presets do this on purpose.
  • AI Video generates motion that did not happen. The subject may appear to move or gesture in ways they never did.
  • Results are synthetic. They are not records of real events and must not be presented as though they were.

Generative models are not deterministic. The same photo and the same preset can produce different results, and some results will simply be poor.
