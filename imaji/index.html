Your photos

What happens to them.

Ordinary editing never leaves your phone. When you choose an AI tool, that one image is sent for processing and the result comes straight back. We don't store it, we don't train on it, and we don't do facial recognition. Every AI result is labelled as AI-generated, and can be reported from the result itself.
